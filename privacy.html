Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Memories Unlocked is designed to help you save journeys, places, photographs and personal stories. This policy explains the information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information you choose to save

Depending on the features you use, Memories Unlocked may process your email address and account identifier, journey and memory titles, stories, dates, location names, saved place coordinates, photographs, clues and sharing settings. Password authentication is handled by Supabase; Memories Unlocked does not receive your plain-text password.

Location features

The app can use location information for maps, place search and location padlocks. Your device's current location is checked only when you ask to unlock a location-based memory. The app does not store that exact checking position; an unlock record may store the distance from the memory, the reported location accuracy, the memory identifier and the unlock time. Saved memories may contain a place name and map coordinates that you choose to attach to that memory.

Cloud and device storage

Signed-in content is stored using Supabase in the European region used by this project. Uploaded photographs are kept in a private storage bucket and accessed through time-limited signed links. Row Level Security is enabled on the app's cloud data tables. The app can also keep records, recovery copies and preferences in browser or app local storage so your work is not lost during normal use.

External map and place services

When you use maps, place search or place-discovery tools, requests may be sent to services used by the app, including OpenStreetMap tile services, Esri, OpenTopoMap, Photon/Komoot, Wikipedia and the OpenStreetMap Overpass service. These providers can receive information normally sent with a web request, such as your IP address, and the place, map tile or saved-place coordinates needed for the feature you requested.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account sync, preserve journeys and memories, show maps and place context, display uploaded media, support location-padlock features and recover your content across devices. Memories Unlocked does not currently use advertising SDKs and does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Cloud account data is kept while your account remains active unless you delete individual content earlier. You can permanently delete your account from Account & Sync inside the app or from the public account-deletion page. Account deletion removes the authentication account, associated cloud journeys and memories, unlock records, and media files owned by that account. Local app data on the current device is also cleared when deletion completes through the app or deletion page.

Your choices

You can use core parts of Memories Unlocked without registering an email account, choose whether to upload photographs, decide when to use device location, and delete your registered account. Device permission controls can also be changed through your browser or operating-system settings.

Security

Memories Unlocked uses authenticated sessions, private media storage and database access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so the app is designed to minimise unnecessary collection and keep privileged server credentials out of the public client.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the app's data practices materially change. The date at the top shows the current version.
