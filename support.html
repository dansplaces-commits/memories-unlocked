Memories Unlocked Support

Use these steps for the most common release-version issues.

My journeys are not appearing

Open Account & Sync, confirm you are signed into the expected email account, then choose Reconnect & reload my cloud stories. Do not create a second account just to recover existing content.

A photo is not showing

Keep the app online for a moment so it can request the private signed media link. If the story itself appears but a photo does not, reopen the journey or memory and try again before replacing the image.

Location padlock will not unlock

Location is checked only when you tap Unlock here. Make sure location permission is enabled for Memories Unlocked and that your device has a usable location fix. The app will show the distance from the saved place when available.

I changed phones or browsers

Sign into the same registered Memories Unlocked account. Cloud-connected journeys and memories can then be reloaded from Account & Sync.

I want to delete my account

You can delete it inside the app from Account & Sync → Delete account & data, or use the public deletion page below without reinstalling the app.
